KidsVidStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

KidsVidStudio ("we", "us") provides videos, stories and ebooks for children and an AI story creator. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Children's privacy

KidsVidStudio is designed for children, and accounts and purchases are intended to be managed by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. We do not show third-party advertising and we do not sell personal information. Parents can contact us at adamhill8597@icloud.com to review or delete a child's information.

Information we collect

  • Account information from a parent or guardian: email address.
  • Purchase and subscription status. Payments are handled by Apple, Google or our payment processor, and we do not receive full card numbers.
  • Content created in the app, such as AI story prompts and generated stories.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic data to keep the app working.

How we use information

To provide and improve the app, manage subscriptions, keep content safe, provide support, and meet legal obligations.

AI features

Story prompts may be processed by an AI service provider to generate stories. We apply safety filters intended for children. Please do not enter personal details in prompts.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the app (such as hosting, payments and AI processing) and when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

We keep information while an account is active and delete it on request, subject to legal requirements. To request deletion, email adamhill8597@icloud.com.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date above.
